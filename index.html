Korean Numbers That Click

Learn Korean numbers as natural chunks: 세 명, 두 시, 십오 분, 삼 년, 네 달, 육 개월, 오천 원. First you practise with support. Then you test what has started to click.

How this works

Korean has two sets of numbers, but you do not need to stop and classify them every time you speak. Learn the number together with the unit or counter that naturally goes with it.

Part 1 · Guided practice15 questions with a short explanation before you answer.
Part 2 · Quiz15 questions without the preview.
Every question gives you an explanation after you answer. You can also go back to completed questions and review your answer.
Before you answer:

Your number instinct is getting stronger.

The goal is not to recite number-system rules. It is for combinations like 스물세 살, 삼십 분, 이백 원, 사천 원, 삼 년, 네 달, 오 개월 to begin sounding familiar as complete chunks.
